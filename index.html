1. Time Constant (\( \tau = 1/a \))

1.00 s

The time required to reach \( 63.2\% \) of the final steady-state value:

\( c(\tau) = 1 - e^{-a(1/a)} = 1 - e^{-1} \)
\( = 1 - 0.368 = 0.632 \; (63.2\%) \)
• Initial slope line: slope \( c'(0) = a \). Intersects \( c=1 \) at exactly \( t = 1/a \).

2. Rise Time (\( T_r \))

2.20 s

Time required to rise from 10% to 90% of the final response:

\( 1 - e^{-at_{10}} = 0.1 \implies t_{10} = \frac{-\ln(0.9)}{a} \approx \frac{0.11}{a} \)
\( 1 - e^{-at_{90}} = 0.9 \implies t_{90} = \frac{-\ln(0.1)}{a} \approx \frac{2.31}{a} \)
\( T_r = \frac{2.31}{a} - \frac{0.11}{a} = \frac{2.20}{a} \)

3. Settling Time (\( T_s \))

4.00 s

Time required to reach and stay within 2% tolerance band of final value (\( c \ge 0.98 \)):

\( 1 - e^{-at_s} = 0.98 \implies e^{-at_s} = 0.02 \)
\( at_s = -\ln(0.02) = 3.912 \approx 4 \)
\( T_s = \frac{4}{a} = 4\tau \)
